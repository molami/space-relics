# Space Relics 🚀

**An Interactive Virtual Museum of NASA's Abandoned Equipment on the Moon and Mars**

Space Relics is an interactive virtual museum developed for the **2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge**, under the challenge **"Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars."**

The project takes visitors on an immersive journey from Earth to the Moon and Mars, where they discover historic NASA spacecraft, rovers, and scientific instruments left behind after their missions.

Through interactive exploration, visual storytelling, and multilingual accessibility, Space Relics introduces learners of all ages to the equipment that helped advance space exploration.

## The Idea

When people think about space exploration, they often remember the astronauts, discoveries, and successful missions. However, the machines that made many of these achievements possible are sometimes overlooked, especially when they are no longer operational.

Space Relics was created to tell the stories of these machines and highlight their contributions to our understanding of space.

The project follows three principles:

**Explore. Discover. Learn.**

## How It Works

1. **Begin the Mission:** Visitors start outside Earth on a space deck before travelling to the Moon.
2. **Explore the Moon:** Three discovery markers are placed across an interactive lunar environment.
3. **Scan and Discover:** Selecting a marker activates a scanning animation and reveals information about a piece of NASA equipment.
4. **Continue to Mars:** After discovering the three lunar relics, visitors travel to Mars to explore three additional pieces of equipment.
5. **Return to Earth:** Once all six relics are discovered, visitors return to Earth and receive a mission completion summary.

Each equipment card includes its name, image, mission details, location, operational status, historical story, fun fact, and a link to a relevant NASA source.

## Featured NASA Equipment

### Moon

- **Surveyor 3:** A robotic spacecraft that studied the lunar surface before astronauts arrived.
- **Apollo 15 Lunar Roving Vehicle:** An electric rover that enabled astronauts to explore greater distances on the Moon.
- **Apollo 14 ALSEP:** A collection of scientific instruments deployed to study the lunar environment.

### Mars

- **Opportunity Rover:** A robotic rover that explored Mars for nearly 15 years and discovered evidence of ancient water.
- **InSight Lander:** A stationary lander that investigated the interior of Mars through seismic measurements.
- **Ingenuity Mars Helicopter:** The first aircraft to achieve powered, controlled flight on another planet.

## Running the Project Locally

To run Space Relics on your computer:

**1. Clone the repository**

```bash
git clone https://github.com/molami/space-relics.git
```

**2. Navigate into the project directory**

```bash
cd space-relics
```

**3. Install dependencies**

```bash
npm install
```

**4. Start the development server**

```bash
npm run dev
```

Open the local URL displayed in your terminal to view the application.
